Project Overview

Architectural Excellence Meets Modern Luxury

Discover a world where architectural brilliance meets nature. Godrej Regal Pavilion offers luxury residences designed with contemporary layouts, exceptional amenities, and unparalleled connectivity in Rajendra Nagar, Hyderabad. Location Advantage

Why Rajendra Nagar Is Hyderabad's Smartest Address

Rajendra Nagar is no longer just a locality — it has become one of Hyderabad's fastest-rising residential corridors, offering the rare balance of greenery, connectivity, and premium infrastructure.

Airport Connectivity

20–25 minutes from Rajiv Gandhi International Airport via the Outer Ring Road — ideal for frequent flyers and business professionals.

IT Hub Proximity

HITEC City, Gachibowli, and Nanakramguda — Hyderabad's primary tech corridors — are all within a 20-minute commute.

Green & Serene Environment

Overlooks the 645-acre PJTSAU University campus — vast greenery giving residents a rare sense of open skies and clean air.

Top Schools & Colleges

Delhi Public School, Oakridge International, Aga Khan School, and multiple engineering colleges within a short drive.

World-Class Healthcare

Apollo (DRDO), KIMS, Care Hospitals, Owaisi Hospital — premium medical care is never far when it matters most.

Superb Infrastructure

The newly inaugurated Dr. Manmohan Singh Flyover (Aramghar–Zoo Park) — Hyderabad's 2nd largest — dramatically improves commute times.

Luxury Shopping & Multiplex

Aparna Mall at Satamrai (facing the highway at Aparna Altius) is the nearest mall, alongside Mantra Mall and Smartzone shopping centre at Vattepally road. Cinepolis multiplex in Mantra Mall, Upparpally, is the nearest multiplex, with Forum Mall currently under construction nearby.

Transport Connectivity

Umdanagar railway station is the nearest station around 5 km away, with DEMU and MMTS local trains running regularly to Secunderabad, Lingampally, and Kacheguda junctions. Gagan Pahad Bus Stand is the nearest bus stop, approximately 5 km from the project.

EV Charging Stations

Electric Vehicle charging stations are available in Shamshabad, at Old Kurnool Highway corner before Aramghar crossing, Shivrampally road (near Purvankara housing society), and at Masjid Ameena, Kurmulguda.

High Court Relocation

The Telangana High Court is shifting to a spacious location in Budwel, Rajendra Nagar, with advocates demanding 50 acres of nearby land as housing for judges and staff, along with a metro line to the High Court.

Resident Stories

"I Walked In to Just Look. I Walked Out a Homeowner."

Priya Venkataraman, a principal architect in Hyderabad, shares how she fell in love with Godrej Regal Pavilion's design and booked her dream home on the very first visit.

"

"My husband said I was being impulsive. I told him some decisions don't need a spreadsheet — they need a feeling. And the moment I stepped into the Godrej Regal Pavilion sales gallery, I had that feeling."

My name is Priya Venkataraman. I'm a 38-year-old architect by training, which means I have spent nearly two decades professionally judging spaces. I know when a building is designed with intention and when it is just built to sell. So when colleagues began whispering about a new Godrej project in Rajendra Nagar, I was curious, but not convinced.

We arrived at the project site on a Sunday at 11 AM. The first thing I noticed — and I say this as someone trained to notice these things — was the land. The sheer scale of the open space between the towers. In a city where every developer is squeezing every square foot, here was a project where someone had deliberately said: leave room to breathe.

"In a city where every developer squeezes every square foot, here was a project where someone had deliberately said — leave room to breathe."

— Priya Venkataraman, Homeowner

As an architect, I walked in with my critical eye fully engaged. I was looking for the compromises. The awkward column in the living room. The bathroom that is really a glorified cupboard. The balcony you can't actually stand in. But they were not here. The living room had genuine width. The master bedroom had a walk-in wardrobe that was actually a walk-in. The kitchen was designed for a person who actually cooks. Even the 10-foot ceiling height was generous without being theatrical.

We had been in the apartment for about 25 minutes when the sales executive led us to the master bedroom balcony. The view that morning was remarkable — an expanse of green, the manicured landscaping below, and beyond it, the skyline of a city that has suddenly remembered how to dream big. I turned to Vikram and said: "we're not going home without booking this."

P

Priya Venkataraman

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Parents & Buyers Ask Us

Quick, honest answers to the questions we hear most from families considering Godrej Regal Pavilion. Have a specific question not listed? Drop it in the form below — we usually reply within 24 hours.

My children are aged 8 and 13. Are good schools nearby, and will they get admission?

Yes — Rajendra Nagar has some of Hyderabad's most reputed schools within a 5–15 minute drive of the project:

  • Delhi Public School (DPS), Rajendra Nagar — excellent for both primary (Class 1–5 for your 8-year-old) and middle/secondary (Class 6–10 for your 13-year-old)
  • Oakridge International School — IB & CBSE curriculum, strong middle-school program
  • Aga Khan Academy — IB curriculum, known for holistic education
  • GMR Chinmaya Vidyalaya and Ravindra Bharathi School — solid mid-tier options

For mid-session moves, DPS and Oakridge accept transfer admissions subject to seat availability — we recommend applying before June for the academic year starting in June. Connect with us and we'll share the latest admission cycle and contact details of school admissions offices.

In case of a medical emergency, which hospitals are nearby?

You'll have access to some of Hyderabad's top multi-specialty hospitals within a 15–25 minute drive:

  • Apollo Hospitals, DRDO (Kanchanbagh) — 25 min, multi-specialty with 24/7 emergency
  • KIMS Hospitals — reputed chain, multi-specialty care
  • Care Hospitals — emergency & trauma care
  • Owaisi Hospital & Research Centre (DMRL Cross Roads, Santosh Nagar) — 26 min
  • Usha Mohan Hospital (Hyderguda, Attapur) — closer general hospital for everyday needs

Ambulance services from these hospitals cover the Rajendra Nagar corridor, and the project itself has 24/7 security + an in-house medical response tie-up.

What entertainment and shopping options are close by?

Plenty — Rajendra Nagar has matured into a fully serviced residential corridor:

  • Aparna Mall, Satamrai — nearest mall, on the highway near Aparna Altius
  • Mantra Mall, Upparpally — has Cinepolis multiplex, food court, retail
  • Smartzone Shopping Centre, Vattepally Road — daily shopping & groceries
  • Forum Mall — currently under construction nearby, will add another major retail destination
  • Metro Cash & Carry, Best Price, Flipkart Wholesale — for bulk & bargain shopping

Within the project itself, you have a 50-seater private cinema, indoor games arena, rooftop sky lounge, and banquet halls — so weekends can be busy without ever leaving the gates.

How long does it take to reach HITEC City, Gachibowli, and the IT corridor?

HITEC City, Gachibowli, and Nanakramguda — Hyderabad's primary tech corridors — are all approximately a 20-minute commute from the project via the ORR and PV Narasimha Rao Expressway. Financial District and Wipro Circle are reachable in roughly 25–30 minutes during peak hours.

Is Godrej Regal Pavilion RERA approved? What is the RERA number?

Yes. The project is registered under Telangana RERA with registration number P024000991. You can verify this on the official Telangana RERA portal. We strongly encourage every buyer to cross-check the RERA registration, sanctioned plans, and agreement for sale before making any booking — we'll walk you through how to do this during your site visit.

What configurations are available at Godrej Regal Pavilion?

The project offers spacious 3 BHK Premium, 3 BHK Large, 3½ BHK Luxury, 4 BHK Premium, and 4 BHK Luxury apartments — ranging from approximately 1,680 sq ft to 3,350 sq ft. All residences are Vaastu-compliant with thoughtfully designed balconies, master bedrooms with walk-in wardrobes, and kitchens built for actual cooking. Family-friendly amenities include a temperature-controlled infinity pool, full-service spa, fitness centre, meditation & yoga studio, indoor games arena, multi-purpose sports courts, private cinema, children's play area, landscaped gardens, rooftop sky lounge, banquet halls, and multi-level smart parking with EV charging. What is the nearest railway station and bus stop?
